# The Nexus Station

**Local-First Headquarters for Intent-Driven Development**

---

## The Philosophy

There is a disease in modern software.

We call it _slop_—code written without consideration, features shipped without purpose, systems architected by autocomplete. The tools that promised to make us faster have made us _careless_. We generate more, understand less. The gap between what we _intend_ and what we _build_ grows wider with every commit.

The Nexus Station exists because we believe that gap is not inevitable.

Software is not merely material—it is crystallized intent. Every function, every data flow, every constraint is a decision frozen in code. When we write without clarity, we freeze confusion. When we ship without alignment, we propagate entropy into the systems that shape human lives.

The cost of slop is not just technical debt. It is moral debt.

---

## The Architect's Seat

Before the first line of code, there is a seat.

It is the place where you ask: _What am I actually building? For whom? Under what constraints? Toward what end?_ This is the Architect's Seat—the pause between impulse and execution where intent becomes structure.

Most tools skip this seat. They optimize for velocity, treating planning as friction. But friction is not the enemy; _unconscious friction_ is. The Nexus Station does not eliminate the hard work of thinking. It creates a dedicated space for it.

We call this the separation of **Drafting** (the immaterial—planning, alignment, meaning) from **Coding** (the material—execution, implementation, deployment).

Draft first. Then build. The order matters.

## The Promise

### Sovereignty

Your machine. Your keys. Your decisions.

The Nexus Station is built local-first. Your project drafts, your architectural blueprints, your source code—none of it leaves your machine unless you explicitly choose otherwise. We do not require accounts, telemetry, or persistent connections.

When you use the Free Local Audit, you provide your own API key. We never see it. We never store it. We never touch your work.

This is not a feature. It is a constraint we will not violate.

### Privacy Absolute

- Source code **never** transmitted to our servers.
- Drafts sent to the Council Audit are **ephemeral**—processed and discarded.
- No analytics. No tracking. No "anonymous usage data."

If we cannot build a sustainable product under these constraints, we will not build it at all.

### Transparency

When you use the paid Council Audit, you receive the **full transcript** of the multi-agent deliberation—not just the polished summary. You see how each perspective was weighed, where conflicts emerged, how synthesis was reached.

We do not hide the process. The process _is_ the value.

## The Components

### The Nexus Station (The Dock)

The desktop application. Your local headquarters.

- Manages your projects and licenses
- Provides the interface for all VDN Labs kits
- Runs entirely offline for core functionality

### The Genesis Kit (The Drafting Table)

For creating **new** projects with intent.

**The Wizard:** A structured interview that guides you through the essential questions:

- Who is the user?
- What are the constraints?
- How does data flow?

The Wizard produces a `DRAFT_BLUEPRINT.md`—a human-readable architectural specification that becomes the foundation for everything that follows.

**The Free Local Audit:** Using your own API key (BYOK), run a single-shot analysis of your draft against the Light Meter—a framework for evaluating whether your architecture is _true_ (technically sound), _kind_ (serving the user), _useful_ (solving a real problem), and _resonant_ (coherent with its own stated purpose).

Zero cost to you. Zero data to us.

### The Council Audit (The Lighthouse)

For those who want deeper scrutiny.

A multi-agent swarm examines your draft from three angles:

- **Logic:** Is this architecturally sound?
- **Safety:** What are the failure modes and risks?
- **Meaning:** Does this serve its stated purpose?

The agents do not merely run in parallel—they _deliberate_. Conflicts are surfaced. Resolutions are proposed. You receive the full transcript and the synthesized final plan.

This runs on our servers (`lpkernel.agency`) and requires credits. Your draft is processed ephemerally and discarded after execution.

**Optional:** Request an Architect Review, where a human (Jean) adds a final layer of consideration to the Council's output.
